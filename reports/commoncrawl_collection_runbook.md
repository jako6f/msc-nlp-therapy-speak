# Common Crawl Collection Runbooks

The operational collection instructions are split by use case:

- [Single-Year Collection Runbook](commoncrawl_single_year_runbook.md): smoke tests,
  targeted reruns, failure recovery, and corpus expansion batches.
- [All-Years Collection Runbook](commoncrawl_all_years_runbook.md): full configured
  trend/corpus collection across all years.

Use the single-year runbook before launching full all-years collection.
